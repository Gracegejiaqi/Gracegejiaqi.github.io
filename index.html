MBZUAI · Prof. Lijie Hu

Training-Free Mixture-of-Experts Pruning

Developing an ACDC-style iterative pruning method that evaluates candidates through forward passes on the currently pruned model—without downstream labels or post-pruning fine-tuning. The study covers router interactions, groupwise verification, and candidate-group splitting across six MoE families.

  • LFM2.5-8B-A1B: 62.22% → 65.93% macro accuracy; C4 perplexity 16.465 → 15.601.
  • OLMoE-1B-7B: 41.44% → 51.67% macro accuracy; C4 perplexity 15.184 → 14.290.

First-author manuscript in preparation for an ICLR workshop.

02

Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Prof. Xiaodong Gu

Feb — May 2026

Repository QA for Software Issue Resolution

Evaluated ACQUIRE on all 500 SWE-bench Verified issues and built LLM-as-a-judge pipelines to study whether targeted repository questions help coding agents understand a codebase before generating patches.

  • Pass@1: 58.4 → 62.2 with GPT-5-mini and 66.4 → 70.8 with DeepSeek-V3.2.
  • Efficiency: QA injection reduced total agent rounds by 17.1%; locating and fixing rounds fell by 23.8% and 24.1%.

03

Technion · Supervisor: Tal Amir

Summer 2026

FSW Embeddings for Document Classification

Embedded distributions over 300-dimensional fastText vectors into Euclidean space using Fourier Sliced-Wasserstein representations, replacing exact Word Mover’s Distance with fast Euclidean kNN on Amazon, Classic, and Reuters.

  • Speed: about 0.02 seconds per query versus about 3 seconds for exact WMD.
  • Hybrid approach: shortlist plus exact-WMD reranking estimates 4–29× speedups near WMD accuracy.

Natural Language Processing · 2025–2026

Grammar-Based Fuzzing for Metamorphic Testing of NLP Models

Designed grammar-based fuzzing to automatically generate multi-step perturbation chains and metamorphic properties, then used CheckList to execute and evaluate INV/DIR tests. A nine-step case study on 100 Twitter airline posts exposed label flips, directional violations, and the threat of semantic drift.
